Local · Private · Reads Anything Aloud
Any input
A URL, pasted text, or a PDF — Genie Reader extracts the article and gets it ready to read.
Three reading styles
Verbatim, a natural podcast-style rewrite, or a condensed summary — your call each time.
8 voices
American and British accents, female and male, each previewable before you commit.
100% local
Article rewriting and text-to-speech both run on-device via Ollama and Kokoro — nothing leaves your Mac.
Reading history
Every reading is saved locally with its audio and script, ready to replay or download offline.
Light & dark
Matches your system appearance, with a one-click toggle whenever you want to switch.
Installing: open the downloaded .dmg and drag Genie Reader into your Applications folder. Genie Reader also expects Ollama for the article rewriting step; it'll offer to install this automatically on first run if it's missing.
